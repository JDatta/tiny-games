Joydip Datta ("we", "us", or "our") provides Number Garden, an offline-first
math learning game for children ages 3–8. This policy describes how the
Android app handles information.

## Information stored on the device

Number Garden does not require an account, name, email address, phone number,
location, photos, microphone, contacts, or cloud backup. The game stores its
own game settings and progress on the device, including the selected level,
coins, milestones, sound preference, difficulty choice, and launch choice.
This information is used only to keep the game working and is not sent to us
by the game.

You may clear this local information by using the game's reset option or by
clearing the app's storage in Android settings. Clearing it resets local game
progress and preferences.

## Optional analytics

The Android version will ask a parent or guardian for permission before loading
Google Analytics. If permission is not given, the Google Analytics tag is not
loaded and the app does not send analytics information to Google.

If permission is given, Google Analytics may receive limited game-use and
technical information. The app's custom analytics events report the game
version, operation type, score, number of answered questions, and the
arithmetic operands and result for a completed problem. Google may also process
technical information that its tag receives when it is loaded. Google processes
this information under its own privacy terms. We use analytics only to
understand aggregate game use and improve Number Garden; we do not use it for
advertising, sell it, or use it for personalised advertising.

Parents or guardians may withdraw analytics permission at any time in the
app's Settings. Withdrawing permission stops future loading of Google
Analytics. [Confirm whether existing local consent state is cleared and how a
currently loaded tag is handled before publication.]

## Children

Number Garden is intended for children. We do not knowingly ask children to
provide personal information. A parent or guardian must make the optional
analytics choice. The game remains fully usable when analytics permission is
declined.

## Advertising, purchases, and sharing

Number Garden does not display ads, offer in-app purchases, or sell or rent
information. We do not share game-progress information with schools, other
users, or advertising partners.

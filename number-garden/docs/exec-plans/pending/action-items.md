## Field definitions

- **Severity:** `Critical` can block release, mathematical correctness, child privacy, signing integrity, or production safety; `High` blocks required QA/compatibility or creates substantial release risk; `Medium` affects release quality or store readiness.
- **Priority:** `P0` = do now; `P1` = required before release-candidate freeze; `P2` = required before or during Play testing; `P3` = production/rollout closeout.
- **Play Store blocker:** `YES` means the task or its documented disposition must be complete before production release; `CONDITIONAL` means it blocks only when the corresponding Play requirement or test finding applies.
- **Status:** `TODO` is actionable now; `BLOCKED` needs listed prerequisites or unavailable infrastructure; `WAITING OWNER` needs an owner-controlled decision/action or explicit external authorization.
- **Owner:** the role expected to close the item. `Engineering` includes coding and deterministic tests; `QA` includes device/browser evidence; `Owner` means Joydip Datta or an explicitly authorized delegate.
